# Historical source catalog

## Research benchmark

**Banacos, Loconto & DeVoir (2014), _Snow Squalls: Forecasting and Hazard Mitigation_**

https://www.weather.gov/media/btv/research/Snow%20Squalls%20Forecasting%20and%20Hazard%20Mitigation.pdf

The paper's Table 2 contains 36 events from 2002–2011 across KBTV, KMPV, and KMSS. Six are marked hybrid. The study manually confirmed narrow convective-band structure using radar rather than broad stratiform precipitation.

## Modern BTV documented episodes

**NWS Burlington, Winter 2018-19 newsletter**

https://www.weather.gov/media/btv/Newsletter/The%20Four%20Seasons%20-%20Winter%202018-19.pdf

Documents the 21 November 2018 frontal snow-squall episode and six Snow Squall Warnings.

**NWS Burlington / NWS Gray, Snow Squalls of 2/27/22**

https://storymaps.arcgis.com/stories/846e970d71b740ffaf6574bf62371dba

Documents the 27 February 2022 episode, eight BTV Snow Squall Warnings, radar evolution, MPING observations, near-zero visibility reports, and snow-squall parameter guidance.

## Operational warning archive

**Iowa Environmental Mesonet VTEC**

https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/vtec/

The IEM archive provides high-fidelity NWS VTEC event records and exposes Snow Squall (SQ) / Warning (W) as an explicit VTEC category.

**IEM API documentation**

https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/api/

The project will use the VTEC JSON service to inventory the post-2017 BTV warning era. Warning inventory is not treated as complete event truth until surface/radar evidence is linked.
